About us

On a mission to transform healthcare

We know that pharmacy needs to change. We are tired of waiting for change. We seek to be the change, because transformation starts with ourselves.

Our purpose

Helping pharmacists chart their own paths

Our purpose is to help pharmacists break out of the self-limiting beliefs that keep us stuck and our profession stagnant.

We envision a world where pharmacists can show up authentically, embrace what makes them unique, and feel unafraid to chart their own paths and challenge the status quo: a united community of like-minded individuals who are inspired to take action and control of their careers, and drive positive change for our profession.

Satsuma is a safe space, a judgement-free zone. A place of self-discovery and innovation, a place to grow as a professional, gain new skills, and find support along the way.

Our team

Pharmacists who've walked the path

Terri Wang, PharmD, PMP

Founder & Coach

Terri started her career in retail pharmacy and has forged her own path one step at a time. Her journey includes roles in retail pharmacy, healthcare IT, consulting, Amazon, and most recently as Chief of Staff for a global healthcare technology company. Throughout her career, she has broken the mold and deviated from the path dictated to her, challenging what it means to be a pharmacist.

Passionate about empowering fellow pharmacists, Terri is committed to guiding professionals to find their own paths, push past comfort zones, and explore new opportunities. She aims to support pharmacists in achieving greater professional satisfaction and making impactful changes in the field.

Mengdi Lin, PharmD

Team Member

After 12 years with Walgreens, I realized that it was not what I wanted to do anymore. Having learned the idea of financial independence, I was able to break the golden handcuffs and start building the life I envisioned for myself.

I know there are many other pharmacists ready to pivot but don't know where to start. Terri can help you find the career that best fits your lifestyle, like she did for me.
