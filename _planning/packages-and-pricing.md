## The packages

### 1. Discovery Conversation: free, 30 minutes

**Who it's for:** anyone considering working with Terri.
**What happens:** a relaxed conversation about where they are, what's prompting the change, and whether Terri is the right fit. Terri recommends a package only if it fits.
**Why free:** it's the main way people decide to buy coaching. Requiring a short application first (see "Making it boutique") keeps these calls to serious prospects.

### 2. AND Clarity Session: $395

**Who it's for:** pharmacists who feel stuck and want direction before committing to a longer program.

**Includes:**
- Pre-work: the Find Your AND assessment plus a short questionnaire
- One 90-minute private session with Terri
- A written one-page "AND map" afterwards: strengths, two or three directions worth exploring, and first next steps
- One follow-up email check-in two weeks later

**Why this price:** specialized career coaches in major metros typically charge $220–$400 an hour. A 90-minute session plus a written plan sits at the upper end of that range, which suits Terri's background.

**Tip:** credit the full $395 toward The AND Program if the client upgrades within 30 days. It makes this an easy first step that leads into the signature program.

### 3. The AND Program: $2,750 (signature)

**Who it's for:** pharmacists ready to make a real move, whether into health tech, industry, leadership or a reshaped role.

**Includes:**
- Six 60-minute private sessions over three months (every other week)
- Messaging or email access to Terri between sessions (for example, replies within two business days)
- The AND map (as above) to start
- A personalized career strategy with target roles and a 90-day action plan
- A résumé and LinkedIn review by Terri
- Interview and networking preparation as needed
- Warm introductions from Terri's network, where it genuinely fits

**Payment:** $2,750 in full, or three monthly payments of $950 ($2,850 total).

**Why this price:**
- Six sessions billed hourly at specialized rates ($220–$400) would come to $1,320–$2,400, *before* the between-session access, written strategy, résumé review and introductions.
- The median US pharmacist earns about $140,910 a year (BLS, May 2025). $2,750 is about 2% of that, so a single raise or better-fitting role repays it.
- It stays clearly below executive-coaching territory, which keeps it reachable for staff pharmacists.

### 4. AND Leadership: $5,500

**Who it's for:** experienced pharmacists aiming for leadership, operations or senior health tech roles. This is where Terri's Chief of Staff and Amazon experience matters most.

**Includes everything in The AND Program, plus:**
- Twelve 60-minute sessions over six months
- Leadership positioning: how to tell your story for director-level and cross-functional roles
- Mock interviews for senior roles
- Offer and salary negotiation support
- A 90-day plan for the first months in the new role

**Payment:** $5,500 in full, or six monthly payments of $975 ($5,850 total).

**Why this price:** executive and specialized coaching typically runs $220–$550 an hour. Twelve sessions at those rates is $2,640–$6,600 before the extras. Keep this tier to two or three clients at a time.

### 5. Flip the Script small-group cohort: $995 founding price

**Who it's for:** pharmacists who want structure and community at a lower price than private coaching.

**Includes:**
- Eight weekly 90-minute live group sessions with Terri
- One 30-minute private kickoff call with Terri
- A small peer group of 6–10 pharmacists
- Workbook and exercises built around finding your AND
- A private group chat for the length of the cohort

**Pricing:**
- **Founding cohort: $995.** Framed honestly as a founding rate for the first group, who help shape the program.
- **Later cohorts: $1,295.**
- Possible early-bird rate for later cohorts: $1,095 if they enroll two weeks before the start date.

**Why this price:** group coaching programs of 8–12 weeks commonly run $997–$1,898 per person, so $995 is a fair entry point for a first cohort. With 8 participants at $995, the cohort brings in $7,960 for about 16 hours of Terri's live time (12 hours of group sessions plus eight 30-minute kickoff calls).

## Making it boutique

- **Apply, don't book.** Replace "Book a discovery call" with "Apply to work with me": a short form with three or four questions about their role, what's prompting the change, and what they hope to build. Terri then invites a good fit to a Discovery Conversation.
- **Cap capacity, and say so if it's true.** For example, eight AND Program clients and two or three Leadership clients at a time. Only state a number Terri will actually hold to.
- **Don't post a full price grid on the site.** A common boutique approach is "Private coaching from $395" plus "Investment discussed in your Discovery Conversation." Publish the cohort price, since group buyers expect it.
- **Name the extras clearly.** Direct access to Terri and warm introductions are what a large coaching company can't offer. Only include extras she'll really provide.
- **Create an alumni circle.** For example, a free quarterly group call for past clients. It costs Terri little time and keeps referrals coming.

## Policies to decide

- **Guarantees:** avoid promising outcomes such as "a new job in 90 days." A satisfaction policy is safer: for example, a full refund after the first session if it isn't a fit.
- **Rescheduling:** for example, 24 hours' notice, with one missed session forgiven per program.
- **Program expiry:** sessions in the AND Program are used within four months, and Leadership within eight.
- **Employer reimbursement:** some employers cover professional development. Offering an invoice with a clear program description makes that easier for clients to request.
