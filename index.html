Questions pharmacists often ask

Do I need to leave pharmacy entirely?

No. Our whole approach is built on the AND: your pharmacy degree is the foundation, not the boundary. Some pharmacists move into a new role full-time. Others add something alongside their current job, change practice settings, or reshape the role they already have.

Coaching helps you figure out which of those fits you, whether that's a big leap or a small, well-planned step.

What kinds of roles do clients move into?

It depends on your strengths and what you want your life to look like. A pharmacist's clinical knowledge carries into many areas, including healthcare technology and informatics, leadership and operations, consulting, industry and medical affairs, education, and building a business of your own.

Terri's own path went from retail pharmacy to healthcare IT, consulting, Amazon Pharmacy, and Chief of Staff at a global healthcare technology company. Mengdi left years in retail for a remote position that made room for the life Mengdi wanted.

Is this only for retail pharmacists?

Not at all. Many pharmacists we hear from start in retail, as Terri did, but coaching is for pharmacists in any setting: hospital, ambulatory care, long-term care, industry, managed care, or academia. New graduates and pharmacists with decades of experience are equally welcome.

If you're wondering what else is possible, you're in the right place.

How long does coaching take?

It depends on where you're starting and where you want to go. Some pharmacists want a few focused sessions to get clear on a direction; others want ongoing support through a full career transition.

We'll talk through what makes sense for you on a discovery call, with no pressure to commit.

What happens on a discovery call?

It's a relaxed conversation about where you are in your career, what's prompting you to look at what's next, and what you hope to change. You'll leave with a clearer sense of your options and whether coaching is the right fit.
